# Governance

## 1. Assessment authority

Initial assessment uses three independent AI Fresh-reads:

- GPT
- Claude
- Copilot

followed by **Human adjudication**.

AI reviewers are reviewers, not votes. The Human is the final **Gyoji / Authority Holder** for publication and is not a fourth equal vote.

## 2. Required sequence

The assessment sequence is:

1. canonical audit / readiness
2. candidate discovery and identity cleanup
3. evidence collection + GPT first-pass for all candidates
4. uniform full-field re-evaluation under the common Rubric and Evidence Gate
5. upper-rank external/counter-evidence pass as required
6. final evidence corpus freeze
7. Claude and Copilot independent Fresh-read against the same frozen corpus
8. discrepancy review domain-by-domain against Rubric and Evidence
9. Human adjudication
10. Banzuke publication and Torikumi where applicable

Wave-by-wave Claude/Copilot review is not the canonical process. No independent-review packet is final/frozen before the candidate field, identities and common evidence corpus are settled.

## 3. Fresh-read principle

Each AI reviewer receives the same applicable Rubric and frozen Evidence corpus and assesses independently, without using another reviewer's conclusion as authority.

For each domain report at minimum:

- Capability Level;
- Evidence Level;
- Recognized Level;
- supporting evidence/rationale;
- contrary evidence or material uncertainty.

## 4. Disagreement

Disagreement is not resolved by majority vote. Divergence is returned to the Rubric and Evidence to identify why reviewers differ. Human adjudication then records a reasoned determination.

## 5. Human adjudication

Human adjudication applies published Rubric/Evidence rather than preference, popularity, organizational prestige, GitHub Stars or model reputation. Material decisions should be traceable to public evidence.

## 6. Conflict of interest

Affiliation, maintenance, contribution or other material interest in an assessed system should be disclosed where applicable. It does not automatically exclude a system; the same Rubric and public-evidence requirements apply.

## 7. Monoii adjudication

During the initial phase, evidence-backed Monoii is adjudicated by the **Human Authority Holder supported by the AI evaluation group**. The community may later add independent Shimpan/reviewers through a prospective governance change.

A successful Monoii does not rewrite the prior Banzuke. It is recorded and informs the next Basho as applicable.

## 8. Torikumi

Torikumi occurs only after final adjudication yields **same Rank + same recognized Total Score**. Formal voting is a **GitHub Discussion Poll**.

Torikumi cannot change technical scores or mandatory rank qualification.

## 9. Publication record

Each published assessment should preserve enough information to reconstruct:

- system identity and canonical links;
- division eligibility / Human principal basis;
- evidence cutoff;
- evaluated version/commit where practical;
- related repositories and identity decisions;
- five Capability/Evidence/Recognized levels;
- supporting and contrary evidence;
- independent AI reviews;
- Human adjudication;
- total score and mandatory-rank result;
- final rank and East/West placement;
- Torikumi result where applicable;
- later Corrections, Monoii and Retrospective Findings without erasing history.
